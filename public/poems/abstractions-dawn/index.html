Abastractions at dawn

September 14, 2025

Last night’s wine is still asleep in my mouth. Cranes build in the distance a what I don’t know. Meaning is hungover, dead in the hallways, Lingering in the grey

trace of a trail cut from an awkward silence. I sought shelter in the rhythms of the rain from a shame that had not yet been named. The morning remains free of judgements,

greets me with her blue. She would not exist, she says, without the outline of my mind. I get up, satisfied thinking I can still please her. I lie back down, saddened I am already thinking.

Who’s to say the flowers outside are not pink clouds of dust, perfume manifest in the spire of consciousness where top-down bottom-up processes collide and dance and tease

apart sense, sense meeting non-sense otherwise it’s all––what? What lies behind these words; watch how they glimmer green only because of light. An ethics

is founded upon this phenomenology, rises from the pores and buds of my tongue which I raise like a middle finger to the sky, to whom might be watching, a figure

already creeping their grip over me for breaking the rules. I lick my lips, suddenly aware of time. I get up and piss, steeling myself for tomorrow.
