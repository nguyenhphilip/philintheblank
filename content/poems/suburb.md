---
title: "Suburban Dinner"
date: 2025-11-24
categories: [poems]
draft: false
---

Not much was said aside the usual
comments on the price of beef and lamb,
it never gets any cheaper. The father
shrugs it off with a resigned laugh, the mother
continues eating without lifting an eye
from her plate. Once the dishes are cleaned
the father sneaks into the garage where he learns
about the history of his people while the mother
scrolls through the sale section of an online
shop. The sons go upstairs to play
video games and write poems
in separate rooms. Both are fighting
an unknown enemy. Both are keeping
themselves alive against others
doing the same.
