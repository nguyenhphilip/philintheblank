title: "Chaos"
date: 2022-07-03
categories: [poems]
title-block-banner: false
---
I hold the world
In a single equation.

*Don’t forget to express 
the bifurcations.*

A flap in the wind
A sudden change of heart
I have accounted for
every unknown start.

*But what of love and culture*
*and all the -isms too?*

It is all certain
in the limit of time.

*But until then*
*what will you do?*
