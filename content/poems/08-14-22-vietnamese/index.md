*What's it mean to be Vietnamese-American?*

At the age of two I learned
of loss and shadows, how both
can exist without the light.
You always had to be
two steps ahead Dad always said
if you wanted to survive.
Stories that were never mine
I carried anyways, a mother
and wife missing in action, the rest
a future placed in the womb of America. 
Home was never a roof but a hunger
longing for something more than history.
