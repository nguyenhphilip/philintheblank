They asked for his time
but not how he got there.
Did he stumble? Did 
his body crumble
like a soggy crepe
held up by the sticks
of his legs on his way
to a determined mark
on a map? Or did he
bumble like a bee
all grace no trace
of a single step
with every toe
tasting loving 
the scent
of asphalt?
